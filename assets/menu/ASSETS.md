# v18 menu artwork

- `brimstone-sanctuary.webp`: original background created for this project with the built-in image_gen tool (no fallback CLI). Source PNG is preserved in the task outputs as `menu-v18-original.png`. The WebP is an optimized encoding of that generated illustration, 1672×941, about 132 KiB.
- `ember-crest.svg`: original code-drawn flame-and-wings emblem for Untitled Terraria Bossfight Game. It is not the Calamity Mod logo.
- Sparks and mist are rendered/animated by the game, rather than baked into the background.
- No official Calamity menu logo, background, soundtrack or other official artwork was copied into this menu. Calamity remains an inspiration and is acknowledged in Credits.

## Final image-generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original fullscreen background artwork for the title menu of a fan-made 2D pixel-action game called Untitled Terraria Bossfight Game. Create a premium hand-painted dark fantasy game background inspired by volcanic brimstone wastelands, not a screenshot or UI mockup. Wide 16:9 landscape, ideally 2048x1152. A vast black-red ruined volcanic sanctuary under a smoky burgundy sky, distant jagged ruined spires and black rock cliffs framing the far left and right edges, subtle red molten cracks and a low bed of firelight at the bottom. Mysterious crimson eclipse glow high in the background, restrained and soft. Layered drifting smoke is suggested as hazy atmospheric depth. The central 45% of the image must be very dark, quiet and mostly empty so a large title, original emblem and menu buttons remain clearly legible. Detailed edges, elegant atmospheric lighting, charcoal black, deep wine red and muted ember orange, cinematic but readable. Texture has a slightly retro game-art grain and crisp silhouettes, not photorealistic and not cute. No characters, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks, no menu buttons, no decorative frames. Do not draw dense sparks: the game will animate real embers rising from the bottom over this image.

## v19 title artwork

`title-logo-v19.webp` is the user-approved original transparent title illustration generated with the built-in image_gen tool: flaming tree on the left, ritual flame/sigil on the right, Untitled Terraria Bossfight Game wordmark. Optimized WebP, 2172×724, about 645 KiB. The source and generation prompt are preserved in task outputs (`title-logo-concept.png` and `title-logo-concept-prompt.md`). No additional image generation was needed for v19. The urn, sigils, embers and cinematic lettering are drawn directly in Canvas.
